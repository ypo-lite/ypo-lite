y-pop-config — layout mock

A static mock, not the WinUI app. Data is the shipped y-pop/rsc/y-pop.xml, plus two marked examples. Click rows, add items, drag files from your desktop onto the tree, or try the sample drops. Comments from the file are rows too: use the Comments switch to show or hide them. Spec: y-pop-config/docs/spec.md.
