Add hotkey
Steal from shell registers the combination with a low-level keyboard hook when Windows already owns it (e.g. most Win+… chords). It applies to every keystroke on the machine, so it's off by default and called out per-row rather than buried in an advanced tab.

Mouse gestures

Hold the modifier and use the button anywhere on a window — no need to aim for the title bar or the edge.

Drag

Moves the window under the pointer.

Resize

Switch

Cycles z-order under the pointer. Off — no button assigned.

WinCtrlAltShift
A gesture with no button assigned is off — there's no separate enable switch to fall out of sync with it. Turning "Switch" on here is just picking a button for it.
